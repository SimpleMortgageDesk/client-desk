Support

We are happy to help. Email and say what you were trying to do. We usually reply within a couple of working days.

Before you write: quick answers

I cannot log in

Sign in with the same Microsoft account you registered with. If you set an app password, that is separate from your Microsoft password. Forgotten it? On the login page choose "Forgot your password or user ID?" and follow the steps. If you changed your Microsoft password, just sign in again with the new one.

I lost or replaced my phone

Your records are safe in your own OneDrive. Log in on the new device and they come back. The app on the old phone locks itself and signs out after a period of inactivity. You can also remove the app's access in your Microsoft account under app permissions.

How do I put the app on my phone?

Open the site in your phone's browser and choose "Add to home screen" (Android Chrome: menu, then Install app). It then opens like any other app.

How do I bring in clients or records from another system?

Open Settings, then Backup and restore. You can import a CSV file or an earlier backup. The app matches people already in the app and offers to merge them.

How do I delete my account and records?

Open Settings and choose Delete my account. Your records stay in your own OneDrive until you delete the folder yourself. See the privacy policy for what we keep and for how long.

Appointments are not appearing in my Outlook calendar

This is switched off until you turn it on. Open Settings, then Email and calendar, and turn on calendar reminders. You will be asked to sign in to Microsoft once more to allow it.

Reporting a problem

It helps if you tell us: what you did, what you expected, what happened instead, the device and browser you use, and a screenshot. In the app, Settings has a Support details section that shows the app version and your device type. Please do not send client, tenant or customer details by email.
