Privacy policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

The short version. Client Desk has no server and no database of its own. The records you type in are saved in your own Microsoft OneDrive or Google Drive account, in a folder named after your business, and on the device you are using. We (the people who provide the app) never receive, see or store your records, your clients' details or your account password.

There are no adverts, no tracking, no analytics and no cookies.

1. Who we are

Client Desk is provided by . If you have a question about this policy or about your information, email .

2. How the app works

Client Desk is a web app made of files that run in your web browser. The files are hosted on GitHub Pages. When you sign in, your browser talks directly to Microsoft or Google; nothing passes through a server of ours, because we do not have one.

  • Microsoft account: your records are saved in your OneDrive, in Apps › Client Desk › your business name.
  • Google account: your records are saved in your Google Drive, in My Drive › Client Desk – your business name.
  • A working copy is also kept in your browser's storage on each device you use, so the app works without an internet connection. Logging out removes it from that device.

4. Permissions the app asks for

Microsoft (only if you sign in with a Microsoft account):

  • User.Read: your name and email address.
  • Files.ReadWrite.AppFolder: read and write files in the app's own folder only. The app cannot see the rest of your OneDrive.
  • offline_access: stay signed in without asking you every hour.
  • Mail.Send (optional, only if you turn on emailing): send an invoice or report from your own Outlook when you press Send. A copy goes to your Sent folder. The app cannot read your email.
  • Calendars.ReadWrite (optional, only if you turn on calendar reminders): add your booked appointments to your Outlook calendar. Only the name, time, type, method and place are sent, never your notes.

Google (only if you sign in with a Google account):

  • openid, email, profile: your name and email address.
  • drive.file: create files and folders in your Google Drive and work with the files the app created. The app cannot see any other files in your Drive.

5. Google user data

Client Desk's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. In particular:

  • Google user data is used only to provide the features you can see in the app: signing you in, and saving and loading your own records in your own Drive.
  • It is not sold, not used for advertising, not used to assess credit, and not transferred to anyone else.
  • No person at reads it. It never reaches us.
  • It is not used to develop, improve or train generalised artificial intelligence or machine-learning models.

6. Who else is involved

  • Microsoft or Google store your records under your own account and their own privacy terms.
  • GitHub (which hosts the app's files) may record technical details such as your IP address when your browser downloads the app, under GitHub's own privacy statement. The app's files contain no personal information.
  • If you choose to send a document by WhatsApp, email or Share, it goes through the app or service you pick.

The app loads no outside scripts, fonts, trackers or adverts.

7. Your clients' information

If you use the app to keep details about your own clients, tenants or customers, you are responsible for that information under UK data protection law (you are the "controller"). For example, you may need to register with the Information Commissioner's Office and tell your clients how you use their details. The app is built to help: it keeps each account's records apart, it warns you not to record things such as bank details or National Insurance numbers, and it lets you export and delete records.

8. Keeping it safe

  • Sign-in is handled by Microsoft or Google; the app never sees your account password.
  • The app locks with your app password after a period of inactivity and signs out automatically after longer.
  • Backups are protected with a password you choose.
  • Please use a device with a screen lock and disk encryption, and do not use the app on shared computers. Turn on two-step verification for your Microsoft or Google account.

9. How long it is kept, and deleting it

  • Your records stay in your OneDrive or Google Drive until you delete them, in the app or by deleting the folder.
  • Logging out removes the copy from that device.
  • To stop the app's access, remove it from your account: Microsoft app permissions or Google third-party connections. Your files stay in your account until you delete them.

10. Your rights

Because your records are in your own account, you can see, correct, download and delete them at any time yourself. We do not hold a copy, so we cannot share, change or delete it for you. If you have a question or concern, email . You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office: ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint.

11. Children

The app is for businesses and self-employed people. It is not intended for anyone under 18.
