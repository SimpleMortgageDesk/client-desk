Running a small business means remembering everything

Details end up in text messages, emails, notebooks and spreadsheets. Then something important slips through.

📱
"Where did I put that?"

Phone numbers, notes and agreements scattered across WhatsApp, email and paper.

⏰
"That expired last month"

Certificates, renewals and follow-ups only remembered when it is too late.

💷
"Has that been paid?"

Chasing payments and working out who still owes what.

🧾
"My accountant needs it by Friday"

A scramble every tax year to gather income and expenses.

How it helps

Less admin, fewer surprises

The app does the remembering, keeps the paperwork tidy and gets the numbers ready, so you can get on with the work that pays.

Everything in one record

Each client, job or property gets one page with contact details, notes, tasks and history. Tap a number or email address to send a WhatsApp or email.

Reminders that don't forget

Services, renewals, deadlines, safety certificates, deposits and mortgage deals, plus your own follow-ups, depending on your work. All shown before they are due.

Get paid on time

See at a glance what is paid, due or overdue. Create quotes, invoices and rent statements as PDFs and send them by WhatsApp or email. Late rent becomes a task to chase.

Documents filed for you

Upload a certificate and it is saved in a folder for that property in your own OneDrive or Google Drive, with a clear name and date.

Tax-year ready

Keep income and expenses as you go, and get a report for your accountant. A Making Tax Digital check shows whether HMRC's new quarterly rules are likely to apply to you.

Stay legal, stay on top

Landlords get a "legal to let" check on every property and a repairs log whose costs go straight into expenses. Tenancy agreements are filled in from what you have typed.

Diary, tasks and notes

Book appointments, keep a to-do list for each client or property, and jot ideas in a notebook. Outlook users can add bookings and key dates to their calendar, so their phone reminds them even when the app is closed.

Phone, tablet and computer

Use it anywhere, even with a weak signal. Changes wait on your device and save when you are back online. Add it to your home screen like an app.

Locked when you step away

Your own password protects the app and it locks itself after a while. Encrypted backups are there if you ever need them.

Questions

Good to know

How much does it cost?

Your first 12 months are free, with every feature included and no card needed. We will tell you about plans and prices well before your free year ends, and nothing is ever charged without your OK.

Which email accounts can I use?

A personal Microsoft account (Outlook, Hotmail or Live) or a Google account (Gmail). Your records are saved in that account's OneDrive or Google Drive.

Do I need to install anything?

No. It runs in your web browser. On a phone, use "Add to Home Screen" from the browser menu and it opens like an app.

Can I use it on more than one device?

Yes. Log in on each device with the same account and password, and your records appear on all of them.

What if I lose my phone?

Your records are safe in your OneDrive or Google Drive. Log in on a new device and they come back. The app on the lost phone locks itself and logs out after a period of inactivity.

Is the tenancy agreement legal advice?

No. The agreements are templates for England that follow current government guidance. Always have important documents checked by a solicitor.

Can my accountant see my records?

Only what you send them. The app creates a report as a PDF or spreadsheet that you can email to your accountant.
