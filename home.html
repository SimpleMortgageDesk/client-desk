Questions

Good to know

Do I need to be a landlord or run a business?

No. Home owners and anyone who wants a simple diary and to-do list can use it free. Choose the home owner and landlord option when you register to get your home, cars, reminders and to-do list. Professionals and trades choose their own kind of work.

How will I be reminded?

Reminders show on your Today screen and in your calendar, with anything overdue first. When an appointment is tomorrow, a bar in the app prompts you to send the person a reminder. You can also turn on notifications on your device; they appear while the app is open.

Which email accounts can I use?

A personal Microsoft account (Outlook, Hotmail or Live) or a Google account (Gmail). Your records are saved in that account's OneDrive or Google Drive. It runs in your browser, so there is nothing to install, and you can use it on any device by logging in with the same account.

What does it cost?

Home owners use it free. Landlords and professionals get the first year free with no card needed. After that the price is shown before you pay anything, and you can leave at any time with your records still in your own account.

What if I lose my phone?

Your records are safe in your OneDrive. Log in on a new device and they come back. The app on the lost phone locks itself and logs out after a period of inactivity.

Is the tenancy agreement legal advice?

No. The agreements are templates for England that follow current government guidance. Always have important documents checked by a solicitor.
