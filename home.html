Less paperwork, fewer surprises

Keep the paperwork tidy and let the app do the remembering.

Questions

Good to know

How much does it cost?

Home owners keeping records for their own home use it free, for as long as they like. Landlords get their first 12 months free as a promotional offer, with every feature included and no card needed. After that it is £0.99 a month for 1 to 5 let properties, or £1.99 a month for 6 or more. Gas and heating engineers also get the first 12 months free, then pay £1.99 a month. We will tell you well before your free year ends, and nothing is charged without your OK.

Which email accounts can I use?

A personal Microsoft account (Outlook, Hotmail or Live) or a Google account (Gmail). Your records are saved in that account's OneDrive or Google Drive.

Do I need to install anything?

No. It runs in your web browser. On a phone, use "Add to Home Screen" from the browser menu and it opens like an app.

Can I use it on more than one device?

Yes. Log in on each device with the same Outlook or Gmail account, and your records appear on all of them.

What if I lose my phone?

Your records are safe in your OneDrive. Log in on a new device and they come back. The app on the lost phone locks itself and logs out after a period of inactivity.

Is the tenancy agreement legal advice?

No. The agreements are templates for England that follow current government guidance. Always have important documents checked by a solicitor.

Can my accountant see my records?

Only what you send them. The app creates a report as a PDF or spreadsheet that you can email to your accountant.
