## Full description (max 4000 characters)
MySimpleDesk keeps your paperwork in one place and reminds you before anything runs out.

Built for landlords, home owners and tradespeople, it opens on what needs doing today and keeps your records in your own OneDrive or Google Drive, so they stay yours.

LANDLORDS AND HOME OWNERS
- One list of your residential property, investment properties and cars
- Reminders for gas safety, EICR, EPC, licences and insurance renewals
- Cars: MOT, insurance, road tax and service dates
- Tenants, rent, receipts and expenses for each rental property
- Upload certificates and documents to each property, car or home

TRADESPEOPLE AND PROFESSIONALS
- Clients, jobs and appointments with a calendar
- Quotes and invoices
- Reminders for renewals, certificates and key dates
- Built for plumbers, electricians, builders, gas engineers, driving instructors, accountants, architects, solicitors, mortgage advisers and more

TO DO LIST
- Add daily tasks, repeat them every day, and tick them off
- See today's tasks, reminders and appointments first when you open the app

DATES READ FOR YOU
- Upload an insurance certificate and the app finds the renewal date for you to confirm
- Reading happens on your phone. Your documents are not sent to us.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
- Your records are saved in your own OneDrive or Google Drive
- We never see your tenants', clients' or properties' details
- Log in with your Microsoft or Google account. We never see your password.
- Optional PIN lock, and automatic log out on shared devices

FREE TO START
- Free for home owners. Landlords and professionals are free for the first year.
