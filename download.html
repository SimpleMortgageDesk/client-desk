Download MySimpleDesk

For Windows and Mac. It is the same app as the website, in its own window on your computer.

Download for WindowsWindows 10 or 11 · about 115 MB Download for Mac (Apple chip)M1, M2, M3, M4 or newer · about 113 MB Download for Mac (Intel)Macs made before late 2020 · about 120 MB

Not sure which Mac you have? Click the Apple menu, then About This Mac. If it says Chip: Apple M…, choose Apple chip. If it says Processor: Intel, choose Intel.

Installing it

Windows

Open the file you downloaded and follow the steps. Windows may show a blue box saying "Windows protected your PC", because the app is new and not yet widely downloaded. Click More info, then Run anyway. You then find MySimpleDesk on the Start menu and your desktop.

Mac

Open the downloaded file and drag MySimpleDesk into the Applications folder. The first time, do not double-click it: right-click (or Control-click) it and choose Open, then click Open again. If your Mac still refuses, open System Settings, then Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway. You only do this once.

Good to know

  • You need an internet connection to open it, as with the website. Your records are saved in your own OneDrive or Google Drive, and the app keeps a working copy on your computer.
  • Updates are automatic. The app opens the live website, so you always have the latest version. You do not need to download it again.
  • Log in with the same Microsoft or Google account you use on the website or your phone, and everything is there.
  • Links to other sites open in your normal web browser.
  • Gmail sign-in is not available in this program. Google does not allow it inside programs like this. If you use a Google account, use the website in your browser, or the Android app.

Prefer not to install anything? Open mysimpledesk.co.uk in your browser. You can also ask for help.
