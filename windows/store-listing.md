**Name:** MySimpleDesk

**Short description (max 100 characters):**
A simple diary, to-do list and customer records, with reminders before anything is due.

**Description:**
MySimpleDesk helps you never miss a task, a deadline or a renewal.

Open it and see your day: today's tasks, appointments and reminders on one screen. Anything overdue comes first.

- A to-do list that keeps up, including jobs that repeat every day
- A diary for appointments, with a prompt the day before to send each person a reminder by WhatsApp, text or email
- Reminders for renewals and key dates, such as insurance, MOT, boiler service, safety certificates and deal end dates
- Customers, jobs, quotes and invoices kept together
- A notebook, and documents filed with the right person or property
- Made for home owners, landlords, mortgage advisers, accountants, gas engineers, plumbers, electricians, builders, barbers and beauticians, driving instructors, architects, solicitors and other self-employed people. You only see the screens for your own kind of work.

Your records stay yours. They are saved in a folder in your own OneDrive. We run no database of your customers and cannot read them. There are no adverts.

Free for home owners. The first year is free for landlords and professionals, with no card needed.
